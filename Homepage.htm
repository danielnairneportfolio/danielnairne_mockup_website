About Jamaica


History


The Beginning

Jamaica was first inhabited by the Taino people. The Tainos lived almost like stone-aged people. They mainly
crops such as potatoes, beans, peanuts, and many more. Centuries later, the Spaniards arrived to Jamaica.


The Spaniards

In the year of 1492, the Spanish sailor, Christopher Colombus, found Jamaica in search of riches, where he
found the Tainos. Over the course of several years, he and his crew had seemingly become friendly with the
people. During this time, he made several trips to and from Jamaica, and on the his last trip back to Jamaica,
he turned on the Taino people and destroyed their homes and livelihood in search of riches.


The British

Around the 1600's, the British found Jamaica inhabited by the Spanish, and took over Jamaica from the Spaniards.
It was also at this time where the Slave Trade occured, where the British took people from Africa, and carried them
all over the Caribbean, which then lead to the slave plantations.


The Uprising

A rebellion occured on October 1865 called the Morant Bay Rebellion, lead by Paul Bogle. This rebellion was to end
the slavery that had been going on. Unfortunately, the rebellion was unsuccessful, and Bogle and the rebellion's
other leaders were hanged. His legacy still lives on to this day.


Jamaica's Independence

Several decades later, Jamaica would be given its indepence on August 6th, 1962, after years of slavery to the British
empire, which lead to Jamaica being able to make its own identity, with introductions such as Reggae, Sugarcane, and
many more.


Geography

Jamaica is a tropical country located in the Caribbean. It is host to three counties (Cornwall, Middlesex, and Surrey),
and is also host to 14 parishes. These are all listed below as:

  1. Kingston (The Capital of Jamaica)
  2. St. Catherine
  3. St. James
  4. St. Elizabeth
  5. St. Mary
  6. Manchester
  7. Clarendon
  8. Trelawny
  9. St. Andrew
  10. St. Ann
  11. Hanover
  12. Westmoreland
  13. St. Thomas
  14. Portland

Jamaica is also known for its mountain regions including places such as The Blue Mountains, or even the Cockpit Country.


Demographics

Jamaica is closing in on having three (3) million people living in the country. Most of Jamaica's residents are of African
descent, there are other races of people living in the country. The most popular being Chinese people as a lot of them are
usually here due to work (especially with Jamaica's relationship with China).
